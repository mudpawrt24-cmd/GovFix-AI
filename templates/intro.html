About PM-KISAAN Samman Nidhi

A short introduction to the portal's purpose, guiding principles, and commitment to farmers.

Mission

Our mission is to provide a trusted, user-friendly digital platform that ensures eligible farmers receive financial support promptly and with dignity. We focus on accessibility, privacy, and operational transparency so that every beneficiary can participate without undue complexity.

Principles

  • Privacy-first: minimize PII and protect sensitive data at every step.
  • Transparency: clear processes and audit trails for citizens and officers.
  • Equity: design services to be accessible across languages, literacy levels, and device capabilities.

Vision

To be the national standard for digitally enabling social protection — a resilient system where automation helps scale services while human oversight ensures fairness and recourse.

Long-term outcomes we aim for:

  1. Reduced delays and leakages in benefit disbursement
  2. Improved farmer confidence in digital public services
  3. Strong privacy protections that preserve dignity

How the Portal Helps You

  • Step-by-step guidance for registration and e-KYC
  • AI-assisted diagnostics that never transmit raw PII
  • Officer escalation channels for complex cases
