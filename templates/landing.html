Welcome to PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

An inclusive, secure, and accessible portal to help eligible farmers receive timely Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT) and support services.

Register Now Learn About Our Mission

Our Mission

To empower small and marginal farmers by providing a secure, transparent channel for benefit disbursement, easy registration, and responsive support — all while protecting citizens' privacy with a zero-PII-first design.

  • Ensure timely DBT transfers
  • Provide intuitive registration and e-KYC
  • Protect farmer data and privacy

Our Vision

To build a resilient, trustworthy digital public service that reaches every farmer — reducing friction, preventing fraud, and enabling equitable access to government schemes.
